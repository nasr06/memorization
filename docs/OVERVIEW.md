# Memorization App — Product Overview

## Vision

A cross-platform flashcard app that wraps the Anki ecosystem (.apkg format) with the motivational hooks of Duolingo: streaks, social competition, daily goals, and dopamine-driven UI.

Users bring their own Anki decks (or create cards from scratch) and stay motivated through gamification. No proprietary lock-in — your cards work in regular Anki too.

## Target Users

- Students (language learners, med students, bar/CPA exam)
- Existing Anki users who want better UX and motivation
- People who tried Duolingo but need custom content

---

## High-Level Feature List

### Core
- Import/export `.apkg` files
- Create & edit decks and cards (text, image, audio, LaTeX)
- SM-2 spaced repetition algorithm
- Study session with card flip animation

### Gamification
- Daily streak (with grace period + streak freeze)
- XP system (points per card reviewed)
- Daily review goals (configurable)
- Level system based on total XP
- Achievements / badges

### Social
- Friend system (add by username or QR code)
- Weekly XP leaderboard among friends
- Deck sharing (publish/browse community decks)
- Study challenges (bet a streak, race to X cards)

### Reminders & Habits
- Push notifications: daily reminder at custom time
- Overdue card alerts ("You have 42 cards due!")
- Streak at-risk warnings ("Study now or lose your streak")

### Sync
- Cloud sync across devices (conflict-free)
- Offline-first: full functionality without internet
